Privacy policy

ML Detective is a free data-forensics game. It collects as little as it can, never sells your data, and shows no ads.

Who we are

ML Detective is run by Vedansh Shrivastava, in India. For anything about your data, email vedansh.shrivastavaa@gmail.com. This address is also our grievance contact under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Your code stays on your device

Python runs inside your browser. The code you type and the data you explore are not sent to us while you play. The one exception is below: when you close a case, the code you ran is saved with your solve.

What's stored in your browser

Your browser keeps your guest session, your detective name and settings, and, for up to 30 days, which link first brought you here. These are essential for the site to work, so there is no cookie banner. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, game records and the waitlist, on servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Vercel hosts the website and counts page views.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're not a bot before creating an account or joining the waitlist. It looks at browser and device signals under Cloudflare's policy.
  • jsDelivr delivers the Python runtime and code libraries to your browser. Like any website, it sees your IP address.

Some of these services process data outside India. We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Guest accounts: deleted automatically after 45 days without activity, together with their games, solves, rooms and shared cases. Feedback you sent is kept without your name.
  • Waitlist: kept until you ask us to remove it, or until we no longer need it for early access.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent to emails at any time. Email vedansh.shrivastavaa@gmail.com with your detective name or the email you used. We'll respond as soon as we reasonably can, and within the time the law allows. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

If you're under 18, please use ML Detective only with a parent's or guardian's permission, and don't join the waitlist yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If we start using your data in a new way, we'll ask first.
