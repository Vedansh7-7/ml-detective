The Fridge With Two Witnesses

Tell a genuine second sensor from a disguised copy.

Two thermometers swear 4,000 vaccine doses never warmed up. The inspector has heard perfect alibis before.

Play this case →

The story

Kowhai Valley Pharmacy is the only chemist for eighty kilometres of Otago sheep country. Every winter it holds the district’s flu vaccines in three medical fridges, and every fridge carries two probes, so that if one thermometer lies, the other can call it out.

Last month a fridge in the next town failed quietly and a whole clinic had to recall its doses. Now inspector Dr Priya Raman is auditing everyone’s logs, and pharmacist Hemi Walker is quietly proud of his: every hour, both probes agreed the vaccines were safe.

Priya is less impressed. Her signature decides whether 4,000 doses go into arms at next week’s school clinics or into a hazardous-waste bin. “Two witnesses are only worth something,” she tells Hemi over a lukewarm cup of tea, “if they didn’t rehearse their story together.”

She has pulled three and a half weeks of the fridge logger’s export, hour by hour for all three cabinets, and handed it to you on a borrowed laptop. One thing in it isn’t what the label on the logger claims it is. Find it before she signs.

What you'll practise

  • correlation matrices
  • unit conversion
  • row-by-row residuals

The fault is a relationship: a feature against the target, against time, or against another source. You write real pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn code in a notebook that runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

The data

Hourly readings from three vaccine fridges over 24 days. Each fridge has a main probe and a backup probe placed elsewhere in the cabinet, plus room temperature, door-open time and compressor duty. One thing in this export can’t be trusted. Find it. 1728 rows, loaded as df.

ColumnWhat it holds
reading_idint, unique reading number
timestamptext, reading time (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM, hourly)
fridgetext, fridge F1 / F2 / F3
main_probe_cfloat, main cabinet probe temperature in °C
backup_probe_ffloat, backup probe temperature in °F (a separate sensor in the cabinet)
ambient_cfloat, room temperature in °C
door_open_sint, seconds the door was open during the hour
compressor_duty_pctfloat, percent of the hour the compressor ran
