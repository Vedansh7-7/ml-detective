Eleven Days From Mars

Catch mixed units hiding in one column.

A Mars orbiter keeps arriving lower than planned. Every thruster firing checks out — on paper.

Play this case →

The story

Cartographer-2 has been coasting toward Mars for ten months. In a windowless operations room in Boulder, Colorado, flight dynamics lead Tamsin Okoro watches the predicted closest approach creep steadily downward, a few kilometres every week, with no single burn to explain it.

Each small thruster firing is written into the log by one of two sources: the spacecraft itself, or a ground model run by a contractor across town. The navigation filter swallows everything. Every row looks perfectly routine on its own, and still the drift keeps growing.

Orbit insertion is in eleven days. If the closest pass sinks much further, the spacecraft will skim the thin upper atmosphere and a $400 million mission becomes a very expensive shooting star. Tamsin’s director wants a real answer, not a hunch, by the morning shift.

You get the full burn log for the cruise, every firing from launch to last Tuesday, and a mug that says WORLD’S OKAYEST NAVIGATOR. Somewhere in this log, the story the numbers tell depends on who is telling it. Find what the navigators are being fed.

What you'll practise

  • physics-style ratio checks
  • groupby by data source
  • scatter plots

The fault is a relationship: a feature against the target, against time, or against another source. You write real pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn code in a notebook that runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

The data

Every thruster firing logged during a fictional orbiter’s ten-month cruise to Mars: angular-momentum dumps (AMD), small trims and trajectory correction manoeuvres (TCM). One thing in this log can’t be trusted. Find it. 1150 rows, loaded as df.

ColumnWhat it holds
burn_idint, unique burn number
datetext, ISO date of the burn
burn_typetext, AMD / trim / TCM
thruster_banktext, thruster bank used (A or B)
reported_bytext, which pipeline logged the burn (onboard_log or ground_model)
burn_secondsfloat, total thruster on-time in seconds
propellant_gfloat, hydrazine used, in grams
tank_pressure_barfloat, propellant tank pressure at burn time, in bar
impulse_nsfloat, total impulse delivered by the burn, in newton-seconds
