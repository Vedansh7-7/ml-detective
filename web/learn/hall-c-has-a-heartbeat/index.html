Hard case · data forensics in Python

Hall C Has a Heartbeat

Find a periodic signal buried in noisy sensor data.

Six server racks. Ten days of readings. Every average is textbook, yet disks keep dying in Hall C. Something in there keeps time.

Play this case →

The story

Merlion Colo occupies four humming floors of a windowless block in Singapore's Jurong East. Hall C is its pride: six racks of servers holding the imaging archive of Tanjong Medical Group, eleven years of X-rays and scans that doctors pull up hundreds of times an hour. For the past month, disks in Hall C have been failing three times faster than anywhere else in the building.

Facility manager Tan Mei Ling has swapped drives, re-seated cables and had the chillers serviced twice. Every dashboard says the hall is fine. Tanjong's CIO has now sent a polite, deadly email: explain the failures by the end of the month, or the four-million-dollar contract moves across the causeway. Mei Ling's night technician, Arjun Pillai, insists the hall sounds different at 3 a.m., as if it were breathing.

Mei Ling has exported ten days of half-hourly telemetry from all six racks. The averages are clean. The histograms are clean. Arjun is sure something isn't. Find what the dashboards are smoothing over, and tell Mei Ling where it lives.

What you'll practise

  • np.fft and periodograms
  • autocorrelation
  • analysing each group over time

Every single-column summary looks fine. The fault only shows across columns, inside groups, or over time. You write real pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn code in a notebook that runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

The data

Half-hourly telemetry from the six server racks in Merlion Colo's Hall C over ten days in July 2025, one row per rack per reading. Every summary on the dashboard looks healthy. One thing in this log isn't. Find it. 2880 rows, loaded as df.

ColumnWhat it holds
reading_idint, unique reading id
timestampstring, reading time (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM), every 30 minutes
rack_idcategorical, rack label R01-R06
inlet_temp_cfloat, cold-aisle air temperature at the rack inlet, Celsius
outlet_temp_cfloat, hot-aisle air temperature at the rack outlet, Celsius
power_kwfloat, rack power draw in kilowatts
fan_rpmint, average server fan speed in the rack
load_pctfloat, average CPU load across the rack's servers, percent
