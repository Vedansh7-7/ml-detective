Learn · 15 cases

Data cleaning and pandas practice, one case at a time

Every case is a real-looking dataset with exactly one planted problem: an impossible value, a leaky feature, a unit mix-up, a pattern hiding across columns. Find it with your own Python, right in the browser.

Several are modelled on real data disasters, from spreadsheet software renaming genes to a spacecraft lost to mixed units. Start with Easy if you're new to pandas; Hard cases need grouping, statistics and a little signal processing.

Easy 6 cases

The fault lives in one column. Summary statistics, value counts and a histogram will get you there.

  • Easy Where Should the Chai Go? Clean up inconsistent category labels A chai startup is about to bet its savings on the wrong place. The board deck says so, anyway.
  • Easy The Tuesday Ledger Spot impossible values with summary statistics A yarn shop's weekly orders. The accountant says one thing in here can't be real.
  • Easy The Spreadsheet That Ate Genes Catch values corrupted by spreadsheet software A career-making cancer result, one shared spreadsheet, and 1,400 genes. Something got eaten in transit.
  • Easy The Mangoes Were Perfectly Fine Find a column that carries no information Every truck of Alphonsos arrived at a perfect temperature. So why is Bengaluru sending half of them back?
  • Easy The Year It Rained Backwards Detect missing-value codes posing as real data A tea estate's rain gauges say 2023 was a catastrophe. The tea bushes look suspiciously well-watered.
  • Easy Too Many People on the Beach Check whether IDs are really unique A sold-out beach festival let in more people than it sold tickets to. The gate log knows how.

Normal 5 cases

The fault is a relationship: a feature against the target, against time, or against another source.

  • Normal The Register That Read Ahead Find a feature recorded after the outcome A lost steamer’s passenger list guesses who came home with spooky precision. Paper isn’t supposed to see the future.
  • Normal Eleven Days From Mars Catch mixed units hiding in one column A Mars orbiter keeps arriving lower than planned. Every thruster firing checks out — on paper.
  • Normal The Model That Knew Too Much Spot target leakage before you train a model A salary model scored perfectly on its first try. The CFO doesn't trust perfect.
  • Normal Death Cap, Dinner, or Doubt Find the annotator whose labels can't be trusted A foraging app is about to tell hikers which mushrooms are dinner. Its five teachers don’t all agree.
  • Normal The Fridge With Two Witnesses Tell a genuine second sensor from a disguised copy Two thermometers swear 4,000 vaccine doses never warmed up. The inspector has heard perfect alibis before.

Hard 4 cases

Every single-column summary looks fine. The fault only shows across columns, inside groups, or over time.

  • Hard Forty-Five Percent Versus Thirty-One Untangle Simpson's paradox with a confounding variable Ashgrove admitted 45% of the men who applied and 31% of the women. The student paper has its scandal. The spreadsheet disagrees.
  • Hard Nine Crews, One Storyteller Use Benford's law to spot invented numbers Nine film crews. 2,160 cash claims. Forty lakh rupees over budget. Every receipt adds up, and that is what worries the accountant.
  • Hard Hall C Has a Heartbeat Find a periodic signal buried in noisy sensor data Six server racks. Ten days of readings. Every average is textbook, yet disks keep dying in Hall C. Something in there keeps time.
  • Hard Echoes in the Static Detect near-duplicate records across many columns An unmanned weather rig in the North Sea. Every chart looks clean. The night engineer says it sounds wrong.
