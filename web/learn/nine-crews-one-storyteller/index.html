Nine Crews, One Storyteller

Use Benford's law to spot invented numbers.

Nine film crews. 2,160 cash claims. Forty lakh rupees over budget. Every receipt adds up, and that is what worries the accountant.

Play this case →

The story

Kaleidoscope Pictures is shooting Suryavansh, the most expensive period epic Mumbai has attempted in a decade. To finish on time, producer Farah Mistry split the film across nine location crews, from the dunes of Jaisalmer to the monasteries of Leh. Each crew runs its own petty cash: chai for two hundred extras, a borrowed elephant, a permit to close a ghat at dawn.

Five months in, the film is forty lakh rupees over budget, and the completion-bond insurer has threatened to take control of the production. Every claim has a receipt. Every category total looks sensible. Farah's finance controller, Ramesh Iyer, has done the books for thirty years, and he keeps muttering the same sentence: "Honest money has a shape."

Ramesh has given you every claim filed this schedule, all 2,160 of them, from nine crews in nine corners of the country. Somewhere in this ledger, money was not spent but written. Find out whose pen it came from before the insurer's auditors land in Mumbai on Monday morning.

What you'll practise

  • leading-digit analysis
  • chi-square tests
  • comparing groups against an expected distribution

Every single-column summary looks fine. The fault only shows across columns, inside groups, or over time. You write real pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn code in a notebook that runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

The data

Every petty-cash claim filed by the nine location crews during the first five months of the Suryavansh shoot, one row per claim. The totals look fine and every claim has a reference. Some of these amounts were never really spent. Find out where they come from. 2160 rows, loaded as df.

ColumnWhat it holds
claim_idstring, unique claim reference
claim_datedate (YYYY-MM-DD) the claim was filed
shoot_unitcategorical, the location crew that filed the claim
categorycategorical, expense category (catering, transport, lodging, props, costume, equipment_hire, local_permits, misc)
payment_modecategorical, how the money was paid out (cash, upi, card, bank_transfer)
receipt_attachedcategorical, yes/no, whether a scanned receipt is on file
amount_inrint, claimed amount in Indian rupees
