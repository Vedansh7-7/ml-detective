The Tuesday Ledger

Spot impossible values with summary statistics.

A yarn shop's weekly orders. The accountant says one thing in here can't be real.

Play this case →

The story

Marigold & Thread is a two-room shop in a converted bakery that sells hand-dyed yarn online. Every Sunday night Priya exports the week's orders and emails them to her accountant, Mr. Okafor.

This week he replied with a single line: "Something in here can't be real." Then he left for a fishing trip with no signal.

Priya has 600 orders, a tax filing due Monday, and no idea what he meant. She has asked you to go through the ledger before then. Start where any bookkeeper would: the totals, the ranges, the extremes.

What you'll practise

  • describe() and min/max checks
  • histograms
  • sanity-checking ranges

The fault lives in one column. Summary statistics, value counts and a histogram will get you there. You write real pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn code in a notebook that runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

The data

An e-commerce store exported one week of orders. Something in this export looks wrong -- find it. 600 rows, loaded as df.
